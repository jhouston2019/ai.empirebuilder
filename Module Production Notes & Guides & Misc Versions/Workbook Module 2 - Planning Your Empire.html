Keyword Research Tracker

Goal: Identify 10 "golden" keywords (good volume, low difficulty, high CPC) and 20 long-tail variations that reveal niche opportunities.

Please provide: 1. 30 related keywords someone might search for 2. 15 problem-based queries (e.g., 'how to...') 3. 10 long-tail keywords showing specific use cases 4. Industry-specific variations Format as a simple list I can paste into a keyword tool."

Long-Tail Keywords (Specific Phrases)

Keyword Phrase Volume Difficulty Insight/Opportunity

What to look for in long-tail keywords:

  • "free [tool]" = users want a free tier option
  • "[tool] for [profession]" = niche opportunity
  • "[tool] with [feature]" = must-have feature request
  • "[tool] no sign up" = reduce friction in onboarding

TAM / SAM / SOM Calculator

Purpose: Calculate the total opportunity (TAM), the market you can serve (SAM), and what you can realistically capture (SOM). Review Lesson 4 for detailed calculation methods.

Target: SOM (Year 1) should be ≥ $100,000 for full-time viability

TAM (Total Addressable Market)

Method: Top-Down Calculation

Example Formula

Total Potential Customers × Average Annual Spend = TAM

Example: 47M freelancers × $180/year = $8.5B TAM
